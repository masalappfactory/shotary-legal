1. Scope and responsibility

This Privacy Policy explains how Screenshot Organizer - Shotary handles information in the app, these legal/support pages and support correspondence. In this policy, “we,” “us” and “our” refer to the app's developer, responsible for the processing described here where it acts as a controller. Apple, GitHub, RevenueCat and a service you choose may have separate responsibilities under their own policies.

For questions or to exercise your rights, use the contact address below.

Contact email: masalappfactory@gmail.com

No Shotary account is required. Not having an account does not mean that every identifier, purchase record or website request is anonymous. This notice describes actual data flows rather than promising that no information ever leaves a device.

2. The main privacy distinction

  • Screenshot analysis is local. The app reads accessible screenshots and performs OCR, visual categorization, metadata extraction, search and tag suggestion on your device. We do not receive screenshot originals, OCR text or tags on an analysis server, and do not send them to a cloud AI provider.
  • Some other operations use the internet. Apple's iCloud Photos may download an original; available subscriptions use Apple and RevenueCat; these pages are served by GitHub Pages; a link or sharing action you choose may reach another service.
  • You control access and sharing. iOS controls Photos permissions. Copy, Share and Open actions have separate effects.

RevenueCat is initialized when the app starts to check subscription status and retrieve available products. These requests can occur even if you have not purchased a subscription or opened the paywall. Development builds use RevenueCat's Test Store; distribution builds use Apple's App Store. If products cannot be loaded, the app does not substitute a purchase at an illustrative price.

3. Information processed on your device

Depending on the screenshots you authorize and the features you use, the app processes:

  • Photos asset identifiers, accessible screenshot images, creation dates where available, image dimensions and information needed to load or synchronize assets.
  • Text recognized from screenshots; extracted links, email addresses, phone numbers, dates, prices and currency text; automatic category suggestions and saved analysis evidence.
  • Your chosen categories, added tags, removed-tag suppression information, “not yet viewed” status and local discovery/update times.
  • Indexing state, retry/error information and analysis versions needed to resume unfinished work and preserve previously saved results.
  • Onboarding progress, the once-only Review guide setting, local TipKit display/dismissal state for the Review introduction, current search input and temporary interface/review state. Review decisions are temporary until their corresponding actions are saved or confirmed.
  • Cached image representations used within the app.

Recognized content may include personal or sensitive information appearing in your screenshots, including information about other people. The app does not require sensitive material for subscriptions or support. Use limited Photos access if you want to exclude particular screenshots. A place suggested from text is not the same as collecting your GPS location. Shotary does not request location, contacts, microphone or camera access for these features.

4. Analysis, search and manual changes

Image and text analysis runs using on-device components. The app does not use your screenshots or corrections to train a shared or remotely hosted AI model, identify people through a face-recognition service, or make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. An image containing a face can still be analyzed as ordinary screenshot content; we do not promise that sensitive content is automatically detected or excluded.

Search uses the local index. Search queries and the contents of search matches are not sent to us or to a subscription provider. The app does not maintain a remote search history. Automatic tags and category explanations can be inaccurate and can reflect sensitive information contained in the source screenshot.

A manual category change is kept as your choice during later automatic analysis. Removing a suggested tag removes it from the visible tag list but may preserve a normalized suppression entry locally so the app does not suggest it again. Reorganizing screenshots is not a command to erase all local preferences, manual choices or stored text.

5. Photos permission and access changes

The app requests Photos access through iOS. Full access allows discovery of accessible screenshots throughout the library; limited access restricts discovery to your selection. Ordinary photos are not indexed merely because they are in a limited selection. Denial, restrictions or a selection containing no screenshots can result in an empty collection.

Withdrawing Photos access hides dependent content and stops authorized access, but does not automatically erase every OCR result, category, tag or index record previously stored in the app's local database. When a later authorized synchronization succeeds, records for assets that are missing or no longer accessible can be removed from the local index. Cached displays and system backups follow the separate rules below.

Manage permissions in iOS Settings for Shotary. The app cannot override iOS restrictions, require full access against your choice or determine a photo selection you have not granted. An iOS permission is a technical access control; it is not a blanket authorization for unrelated processing or a waiver of rights under privacy law.

6. iCloud Photos, backups and network use

If a screenshot is stored only in iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos services may need to download it before local viewing or analysis. That communication is between your device and Apple's services under your Apple settings; it is not an upload to a Shotary analysis server. Transfers may wait for connectivity and may use your data plan.

Shotary does not operate its own cloud synchronization or backup service for your screenshot index. However, iOS device backups, iCloud Backup, device migration or other system features may include app data according to your Apple settings. Originals in iCloud Photos and backup copies can exist independently of the app. Removing a live local record does not guarantee deletion from an existing Apple backup.

The app does not continuously monitor screenshots while it is closed or promise scheduled background checks. Any future notification or materially different background feature must follow iOS authorization and updated disclosures where required.

7. Home Screen widgets

This version does not include widgets and does not prepare or export a shared widget cache. If widgets are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe their data handling and controls before they become available.

8. Apple purchases and RevenueCat

When subscription services are enabled, Apple processes checkout and billing. RevenueCat helps verify purchases, restore entitlements and manage subscription status. Information used for these functions may include store transaction/receipt information, product and subscription identifiers, purchase/renewal/expiry/refund events, entitlement status and the technical context needed to serve those requests.

RevenueCat assigns an anonymous app-user identifier even though there is no Shotary login. Such an identifier is pseudonymous, not necessarily irreversibly anonymous, and can be associated with a purchase or restoration. Necessary service requests also expose technical request information, such as the source IP address to the receiving infrastructure, and may include app, SDK, device/OS or store context. We do not send screenshot content, OCR, tags, search queries or extracted email addresses as subscription customer attributes.

We do not receive your full payment-card number or Apple password. We may view subscription records and operational subscription reports to provide access, resolve billing issues and prevent fraud. These are distinct from an advertising profile or a general-purpose record of the content of your screenshots.

See Apple's Privacy Policy, RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's privacy-role information. Apple independently handles its store and payment obligations; RevenueCat acts on our behalf for the subscription processing it provides, while its own independent activities remain subject to its notices.

9. Copying, sharing, opening links and support

When you choose Copy, the selected value is written to the system clipboard. When you choose Share, the selected value is passed to the destination you choose through iOS. When you choose Open, the relevant link or action is handed to its destination. These user-requested actions are separate from local analysis. The destination's collection and any clipboard, keyboard, browser or device synchronization are governed by that service and your system settings.

If you contact support, we receive the email address and other information you choose to send, such as a message, optional attachment, app/OS version or purchase reference needed to investigate. A screenshot sent with a support email is a voluntary disclosure, not part of automatic analysis. Avoid including unrelated private content, credentials or complete payment-card information. We do not need your Apple password to provide support.

Support correspondence is processed by the mailbox provider used for the contact address and, where necessary, the service provider involved in resolving your request. We use it for support, rights requests, security or legally required recordkeeping, not as automatic permission for marketing.

10. GitHub Pages and the legal-document WebView

These legal/support pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security when a Pages site is visited, including when a visitor is not signed in. Serving a page also involves the technical request needed by the host to deliver it. See GitHub Pages hosting information and GitHub's Privacy Statement for the host's handling and request routes.

We add no advertising pixels, general analytics scripts, third-party fonts, forms, login, cookies or browser local-storage code to these pages. This does not mean that the hosting provider has no security logs, or that a different site you open from a link has no cookies or tracking.

In the app, documents open in a WebView configured without persistent website storage and with page JavaScript disabled. Loading the remote page still reveals the network request to its host. Nonpersistent storage is not a promise of anonymous browsing or immediate deletion from the host's logs.

14. How to remove local data or stop a feature

To stop Photos access, change Shotary's permission in iOS Settings. To remove live app-local data, use iOS Delete App; Offload App intentionally preserves app documents and data. Keychain items, system caches, restored backups and provider records can follow different system rules. Restoring a device backup can restore data you previously removed from a live installation.

Deleting the app is not a deletion of your Photos originals, a request to erase Apple's billing records or cancellation of a subscription. To delete originals, use a confirmed Photos deletion. To stop renewal, use Apple's subscription controls. To request deletion of support or subscription data handled on our behalf, contact us separately.

Deleting a RevenueCat customer record, where a lawful request is fulfilled through that service, does not cancel an Apple subscription. A later purchase, restoration or other newly requested entitlement operation can create necessary new records. Any retention or renewed processing must still have a lawful basis. We cannot promise that uninstalling alone contacts every provider to erase information.
