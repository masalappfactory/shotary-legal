1. No screenshots or limited Photos access

Shotary organizes screenshots, not every image in Photos. If you chose limited access, make sure your selected items include screenshots. Use the app's selection-management action when available, or change Shotary's Photos access in iOS Settings. Denied or restricted access can prevent discovery; restrictions managed by a parent or organization may need to be changed by that administrator.

An iCloud-only screenshot may need a network connection before it can be displayed or analyzed. Reconnect and use the available retry action for a failed download. Limited access with no screenshots is an empty selection, not proof that your full Photos library has no screenshots.

2. Categories, search and New

Automatic categories, text, tags and extracted details can be incorrect. Where available, open a screenshot's details to change its category or add/remove tags. Check the original before opening a recognized link or relying on a date or price. Manual categories and tag edits are saved locally.

New means not yet viewed in Shotary; it is not limited to screenshots taken today. The app checks accessible content when opened/refreshed and responds to changes while active. It does not promise instant detection or notifications while closed. Opening New alone does not mark every item seen.

Free access includes All screenshots thumbnails and clear search results. Opening details, categories/Review or New requires a verified Premium entitlement. A scan completed in onboarding does not itself activate Premium.

3. Purchases and Restore

Purchases are available only after subscription products are configured in your app version. Use the actual price and billing period shown by the app and Apple's confirmation sheet. An unavailable or pending product does not mean a purchase succeeded.

If you already bought Premium, use Restore with the Apple Account associated with that purchase and an available connection. Restoration rechecks access; it does not create a new purchase, extend a subscription, or recover local analysis/tags lost when an installation was removed. If access remains incorrect, contact us with the app version and a relevant transaction reference, without your password or complete payment details.

4. Cancel a subscription or request a refund

Use your Apple Account's Subscriptions controls in iOS Settings or Apple's cancellation instructions. Deleting/offloading the app, removing a widget or denying Photos access does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel a trial at least 24 hours before it ends where Apple's rule applies; the offer and local law determine access after cancellation.

For Apple-billed refunds, use Report a Problem and Apple's refund guidance. Eligibility depends on your purchase and applicable law. Contact us for app defects and remedies for which we are responsible. See the Terms for more detail.

5. Delete selections, originals or app data

A Delete decision in Review is a selection until you confirm the final operation and any Photos prompt. Undo can change review decisions before actual deletion; it cannot recover a completed Photos deletion. Check Photos if an operation was interrupted or a local refresh warning appears. Apple controls any Recently Deleted recovery and iCloud synchronization.

Changing permission does not erase all previously stored OCR or tags. To remove live app-local data, use iOS Delete App; Offload App preserves app data. Originals, system backups, shared caches and purchase records follow separate rules. App deletion does not cancel billing. See data-removal information before taking action.

6. Widget previews and refresh timing

Overview widgets show counts; category image widgets require eligible Premium access and locally available previews. Edit the widget to choose an available mode/category. iOS controls timeline timing, so slides do not continuously animate and may remain unchanged while the system delays a refresh.

After changing Photos or Premium access, reopen Shotary so it can update its shared cache. A rendering already cached by iOS may persist. Remove the widget if it must stop showing content immediately. Consider who can see your Home Screen.

7. Contact support or make a privacy request

Contact email:

Include a short description, app/iOS version and the steps that led to the issue. A relevant purchase reference can help with access questions. Attach a screenshot only if needed and remove unrelated private content. We do not need your Apple password or complete payment-card number.

For a privacy request, describe the right or action you want to exercise; see the request process. Local-only screenshot data is not available to us on a server. We can explain local controls and handle support/subscription information under our responsibility.
